# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

KGRTC is an open research project. We expect participants to communicate in a professional, technically rigorous, and respectful way.

Contributors should:

- assume good faith;
- criticize ideas and implementations rather than people;
- welcome independent review and disagreement;
- clearly distinguish evidence from speculation;
- give credit for prior work;
- avoid harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks; and
- help make the project accessible to people with different levels of cryptographic experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment or targeted hostility;
- threats or intimidation;
- discriminatory or demeaning language;
- deliberate personal attacks;
- publishing private information without permission;
- spam or repeated disruptive behavior; and
- knowingly presenting fabricated results as technical evidence.

## Technical disagreement

Strong disagreement is allowed and often useful in cryptographic research.

A disagreement should focus on:

```text
claim → evidence → reasoning → conclusion
```

rather than on the identity or competence of the person making the claim.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project usable and safe.

For security issues, follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

For other conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately through the repository's available contact mechanism.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, issue discussions, pull requests, and project-related interactions where participants are representing the project.
